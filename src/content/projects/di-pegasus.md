## Summary

DI-PEGASUS is an EU Horizon Europe project developing a fully autonomous,
multi-domain, multi-platform system for persistent surveillance and
reconnaissance. The work focuses on coordinating heterogeneous platforms so
they can plan, cooperate and share sensing across domains to maintain
continuous coverage of an area.

## Highlights

- Multi-domain, multi-platform autonomous operation
- Cooperative mission planning across heterogeneous agents
- Persistent surveillance and reconnaissance coverage
- Developed under the EU Horizon Europe programme

## Challenges
- Collaborating with multiple European partners across different domains
- Refining the system to meet requirements from stakeholders
- Tailoring the Multi-agent system solution to different use cases with specific Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

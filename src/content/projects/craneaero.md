## Summary

A guidance stack that steers a UAV to a goal while avoiding static and moving
obstacles, validated in a software-in-the-loop simulation.

## Highlights

- Real-time vector-field guidance
- Velocity-obstacle based avoidance
- ROS 2 + Gazebo integration

## Challenges

- Leading and uniting a team of volunteers to build a complex autonomous system
- Managing diverse skill levels and coordinating remote work
- Concurrent engineering across multiple subsystems
- Bridging the gap between simulation and real-world deployment

## Summary

A NASA RASC-AL competition entry proposing the Portable Utility Pallet: a
concept for coordinating multiple autonomous agents to plan, move in formation
and cooperate in dynamic environments. The design was written up in a full
proposal and presented as part of the competition.

## Highlights Concept

- Wireless power transmission
- Oil-bath cooling battery pack
- Origami-inspired folding mechanism
- Active thermal management


## Challenges
- Coordinating multidisciplinary teams across multiple locations remotely
- Balancing innovation with feasibility
- Managing competing priorities
